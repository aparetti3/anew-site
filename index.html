How it works

Set it once. Then let it find you.

  1. 1

    Pick your moments

    Choose the categories that fit your life right now. Change them whenever it changes.

  2. 2

    Choose when

    Set how often and a window in your day. Anew sends affirmations as quiet notifications inside it.

  3. 3

    Read, then swipe

    Open the app to one affirmation, centred and alone on the screen. Swipe for the next, or back to the last.
